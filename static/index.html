AeroPulse Mathematical & Analytical Methodology

Real-Time Laspeyres Airfare Price Index Specification

1. Laspeyres Price Index Formulation

The platform calculates the domestic Airfare Price Index relative to a standardized base period benchmark (₹6,920) using fixed passenger traffic weights:

Airfare Index = ( ∑ [w_i × P_{i,current}] / ∑ [w_i × P_{i,base}] ) × 100

Where $P_{i, ext{current}}$ is the current weighted fare, $P_{i, ext{base}}$ is the base benchmark fare (₹6,920), and $w_i$ is the route traffic share.

2. Numerical Index Example

• Base Period Weighted Average Fare = ₹6,920
• Current Weighted Average Fare = ₹7,955
• Airfare Price Index = (7,955 / 6,920) × 100 = 115.4
• Interpretation: Airfares have increased by approximately 15.4% compared with the base period benchmark.

3. Explainable Anomaly Detection Algorithm

Z-Score = ( P_{observed} - μ_{route,window} ) / σ_{route,window}

Flags price surges when $Z \ge 1.95$ or percentage deviation $\ge +35\%$ with transparent root cause diagnostics.

About AeroPulse

Standalone Professional Aviation Data Intelligence Platform

What is AeroPulse?

AeroPulse is an independent aviation data intelligence platform that automatically collects, cleans, normalizes, and analyzes domestic airfare movements across India. It computes high-frequency price indices and delivers explainable pricing intelligence for aviation analysts, economists, and researchers.

Who Can Use AeroPulse?

• Civil Aviation Analysts: Monitor route volatility and carrier seat yield behavior.
• Economic Researchers: High-frequency visibility into transportation pricing and travel inflation trends.
• Commercial Airlines: Competitive market benchmarking across routes, advance windows, and distribution channels.
